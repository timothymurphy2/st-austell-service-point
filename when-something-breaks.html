A place for real life

When something breaks

The washing machine stops. The bill arrives. There is no spare money this week. It helps to know there is someone local to talk to before making a rushed decision.

A few hundred pounds can matter

An amount that seems small to a lender can make a big difference at home. A repair, a replacement, or simply getting through the month deserves a careful conversation.

The quick offer has a longer story

Money offered in minutes can take much longer to repay. Before saying yes, it is worth understanding the full cost and what it will leave for everything else.

A question worth asking early

If something broke next month, where would you turn? Come and speak with a St Austell Service Point volunteer. You can find out how to reach Kernow Credit Union and ask what options may be available to you.
