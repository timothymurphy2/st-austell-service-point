A place for real life

The things we know are coming

School shoes. Uniforms. Birthdays. Christmas. We know they are coming, but finding the money all at once can still be difficult.

The year has a rhythm

Some costs return every term or every year. Seeing them ahead of time gives us a chance to prepare, even when there is little room in the weekly budget.

A little, regularly

Putting aside an amount you can manage, whenever you can, can make the next expense less daunting. It is your money, ready for something you knew would matter.

A local place to begin

You do not need a perfect plan before you walk in. Speak with a St Austell Service Point volunteer about joining Kernow Credit Union and finding a way to prepare that works for you.
